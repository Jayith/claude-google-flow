🎬  Google Flow for Claude Code is ready to roll!

    Lights… camera… prompts. Ask Claude for pictures and videos, it makes
    them in Google Flow and drops the files right on your computer.

  ✨ Start here
     /google-flow:setup       2-minute guided setup + a free test image 🤖👋

  🎨 Make stuff
     /google-flow:generate image "a corgi astronaut, cinematic" --count 2
     /google-flow:generate video "steam rising from a coffee" --aspect 9:16
     /google-flow:generate video "make it dance" --start ./robot.jpg

     Options  --ref <img>   --start / --end <img>   --count 1-4
              --model pro | lite | fast | quality   --out <folder>
              --aspect 16:9 | 9:16 | 1:1 | 4:3 | 3:4

  🎟️  Check your wallet
     /google-flow:credits     credits left, refill time, what each model costs

  👋 See this again
     /google-flow:welcome

  💬 Or just ask: "make me a 9:16 Veo clip of a paper boat in the rain"
  💳 Claude always asks before spending credits. Have fun! ✨
